Sunday, May the Tenth

For Her, on Mother's Day

Brunch & Dinner, service all day
Dinner  ·  Our Gift to Mothers Unlimited wine for Mom. Every mother dining with us on Sunday evening pours freely from a curated selection — all night, on the house.
Brunch  ·  A Welcome Pour for Mom A complimentary glass of sparkling wine for every mother at brunch.

Dinner is where the day lands — a long, candlelit service with the room at its best. Whole grilled branzino, duck leg confit, rib eye for two, and a wine list that knows the occasion. Earlier, brunch moves at its own pace: croissant French toast, smoked salmon, eggs Benedict, a bottle of something cold on ice.

The good tables go first. Reserve early.

IV.
The Weekend Ritual

Weekends are for Brunch

Saturdays and Sundays, the room fills up early and stays full. The LouLou Burger, eggs Benedict, the French Toast, a Bloody or two. Sit inside if it's raining; outside if it isn't.

Bring the dog. Bring the friend you've been meaning to call.

V.
The Season Opens

A Cabana on Eighth Avenue

The sidewalk cabanas are open for the season — private little rooms with a view of Chelsea passing by. Available all week, lunch through dinner. Best for long afternoons that turn into evenings, and evenings that nobody wants to end.

Cabanas on 8th, every day of the week. Reserve specifically — they go first.

Adelaide's Salon

The speakeasy below LouLou runs weekly — live jazz, burlesque, handcrafted cocktails, the whole vintage proposition. Inspired by the travels of the explorer Isabella Bird — which is to say, the room takes itself only as seriously as it needs to.

176 Eighth Avenue, after the bistro closes its doors.

The Room, Yours for the Night

Private events at LouLou — the full bistro, the speakeasy below, or somewhere in between. Birthdays, rehearsal dinners, holiday parties, the kind of company gathering people actually attend.
